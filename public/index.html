Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Saba MD.

Is Saba MD free to use?
Yes — the Starter plan is completely free with 1 WhatsApp session and access to all 257+ commands. The Pro plan offers up to 10 concurrent sessions.
How does the pairing system work?
Enter your WhatsApp number, and we generate an 8-digit code in real time from WhatsApp servers. Open WhatsApp → Linked Devices → Link with Phone Number, and paste the code.
Can multiple users use one bot?
Yes — up to 10 different WhatsApp numbers can be paired simultaneously on a single server, each with their own independent bot instance.
What AI features are included?
GPT-4o for intelligent chat, DALL-E 3 for image generation, plus summarization, translation, code generation, roleplay, and brainstorming.
Where can I deploy this?
Anywhere Node.js runs — Vercel, ClawCloud, Render, Railway, VPS, or your own server. Docker and deployment configs are included.
